Stem Cell Regeneration through NAD+ Boosters

April 2023, Biotechnology Category

NAD+, a critical coenzyme for energy production and DNA repair, declines with age. This decline affects stem cell function and regenerative capacity. Research has found that boosting NAD+ levels can enhance cellular metabolism and promote mitochondrial dynamics. Studies have shown that nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide biosynthesis involves nicotinamine phosphoribosyltransferase (NAMPT). Boosters like nicotinamine riboside (NR) or nicotinamine mononucleotide (NMN) provide necessary precursors for this reaction. Elevated NAD+ levels inhibit senescent cells, which are non-dividing, aging cells. A case study in the Journal of Clinical Investigation found that NR improved stem cell transplantation outcomes by increasing NAD+ levels and promoting increased mitochondrial activity. Another study in Blood found that NR enhanced hematopoiesis through increased mitochondrial clearance. **Prostar Life Hack:** Incorporate NR or NMN into your diet and exercise regularly to promote NAD+ levels and enhance mitochondrial activity. Adequate sleep is essential for NAD+ biosynthesis.
