Haritaki And Pine Bark Extract Synergy

MECHANICS

Haritaki (Terminalia chebula) and Pine Bark Extract are both renowned for their antioxidant properties, with distinct mechanisms of action that support various biological pathways. Haritaki is an ancient Ayurvedic herb known for its ability to promote digestive health, detoxify the body, and support immune function. It contains a variety of bioactive compounds such as tannins, flavonoids, phenolic acids, and polysaccharides. These components work synergistically to modulate oxidative stress and inflammation within the body.

Pine Bark Extract, derived from maritime pine (Pinus pinaster), is rich in proanthocyanidins which are potent free-radical scavengers. It has a well-established role in enhancing cerebral blood flow by increasing nitric oxide levels, thereby dilating blood vessels and improving vascular health. Additionally, Pine Bark Extract exerts its anti-inflammatory effects by reducing the production of pro-inflammatory cytokines, which can lead to neuroprotection against oxidative damage.
