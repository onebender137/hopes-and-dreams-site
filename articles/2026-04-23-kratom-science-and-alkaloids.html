KRATOM: THE ALKALOID PROFILE OF MITRAGYNA SPECIOSAKRATOM IS A SOUTHEAST ASIAN BOTANICAL THAT HAS BEEN USED FOR GENERATIONS AS BOTH A STIMULANT AND A RECOVERY AID. ITS EFFECTS ARE PRIMARILY DRIVEN BY TWO KEY ALKALOIDS: MITRAGYNINE AND 7-HYDROXYMITRAGYNINE.MITRAGYNINE: THE FOCUS DRIVERMAKING UP APPROXIMATELY 66% OF THE TOTAL ALKALOID CONTENT, MITRAGYNINE ACTS AS A STIMULANT AT LOW DOSES. IT BINDS TO MU-OPIOID RECEPTORS BUT OPERATES AS A G-PROTEIN BIASED AGONIST, WHICH SIGNIFICANTLY REDUCES THE RISK OF RESPIRATORY DEPRESSION COMPARED TO TRADITIONAL OPIOIDS.7-HYDROXYMITRAGYNINE: THE RECOVERY COMPONENT WHILE ONLY A MINOR CONSTITUENT (<2%) IN RAW LEAF, 7-OH IS SIGNIFICANTLY MORE POTENT AND IS RESPONSIBLE FOR THE ANALGESIC AND SEDATIVE EFFECTS FOUND AT HIGHER DOSAGES. IT IS ESSENTIAL TO USE HIGH-QUALITY, LAB-TESTED LEAF TO ENSURE A SAFE ALKALOID BALANCE.
