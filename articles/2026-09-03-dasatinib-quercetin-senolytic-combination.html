THE MECHANICS

The combination targets senescent cells, a primary culprit in the progression of DKD. Through their synergistic action, DQ reduces the accumulation of these senescent cells, thereby alleviating the oxidative stress and inflammation that characterize DKD. This process is critical in restoring renal function and halting disease progression.

DQ's efficacy in protecting against renal damage is underscored by its ability to enhance the clearance of senescent cells. By doing so, the combination not only reduces the burden of these cells but also ameliorates the microenvironment in which they thrive, offering a multifaceted approach to combating DKD. Moreover, DQ's protective activity extends to the amelioration of oxidative stress, a hallmark of DKD. The combination's antioxidant properties help neutralize reactive oxygen species (ROS), thereby mitigating the oxidative damage that underpins renal injury.
